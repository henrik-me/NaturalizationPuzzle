The first half of the 20th century brought the United States onto the world stage. Two world wars and a great economic crisis tested the country.

## Wars of the 1900s

The United States fought several wars in the 1900s. They include **World War I**, **World War II**, the **Korean War**, the **Vietnam War**, and the **Persian Gulf War** [1].

## World War I

**World War I** lasted from 1914 to 1918. The United States entered the war in **1917**. There were many reasons. The U.S. wanted to support its allies (Britain and France). German submarines were attacking American ships. Germany had tried to get Mexico to attack the United States [2].

## Women Win the Right to Vote

In **1920**, **all women got the right to vote** in the United States [3]. The change came with the **19th Amendment**. Women had fought for this right for more than 70 years.

## The Great Depression

The **Great Depression** was the worst economic crisis in U.S. history [4]. Banks failed. Many businesses closed. About one in four workers had no job. People lost their homes and farms [4].

The Great Depression **started in 1929** with the stock market crash on Wall Street [4]. It lasted into the 1930s.

**Franklin D. Roosevelt** — usually called **FDR** — was president during the Great Depression and World War II [5]. He led a recovery program called the **New Deal**.

## World War II

**World War II** ran from 1939 to 1945. The United States entered the war in **December 1941** after **Japan attacked Pearl Harbor** in Hawaii [6]. Germany and Italy then declared war on the United States.

The U.S. fought on the side of the **Allies** — Britain, the Soviet Union, France, and others — against the Axis (Germany, Italy, and Japan) [6]. The war ended in 1945. Millions of people died around the world.
